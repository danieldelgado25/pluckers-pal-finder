# Simplify sauces and strengthen wing combos

## Changes
- Replace founder-name sauce navigation with three useful choices: Pluckers Originals, Traditional Flavors, and Dry Rubs.
- Fold Mark’s, Dave’s, and Sean’s picks into the matching sauce sections instead of presenting them as separate categories.
- Mark each founder pick directly on its sauce card with a distinctive “Mark’s Pick,” “Dave’s Pick,” or “Sean’s Pick” treatment so it feels special without interrupting browsing.
- Keep Pickle Dust as the standalone featured sauce.
- Rework the Wings view so 5, 10, and 15-piece base orders lead visually, each with a prominent combo upgrade showing its included side and combo price.
- Keep the 20-piece, challenge, and party platter options as separate items below the main wing choices.
- Apply the same structure and labels in English and Spanish.

## Technical details
- Preserve the current menu and pricing data while adding founder-pick metadata to sauces.
- Use existing semantic red, yellow, and blue design tokens and the shared flame rating component.
- Verify both pages at desktop and mobile sizes, including tab and jump-button behavior.
